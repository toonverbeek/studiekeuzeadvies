# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary: the study chooser, 16 to 22.** Two groups of almost equal size, and the old site proves it:

- *The first chooser.* An exam pupil at MBO, HAVO, or VWO who must select a study and does not know how. Too many options, or no idea at all. They read on a telephone, often late, often after a conversation at home that did not help.
- *The stopper.* About half of the old customers. They started a study, it was wrong, and they stopped or want to stop. They feel they failed. They need a plan, not a test.

A third group is named on the old site: choosers with ADD, ADHD, or autism, who need overview more than options.

**Secondary: the parent.** The parent pays, the parent searches too, and the parent decides whether outside help is worth €269 or €699. They want other information than the child: what happens in the four meetings, what it costs, what the result is, and who the coach is.

**Third: the coach.** The business is a network of freelance study choice coaches per city. Without coaches there is no product. The site must make a good coach want to write to us, in their own city.

## Product Purpose

A Dutch site for study choice advice, rebuilt after the domain, the content, and the search rankings were bought from Qompas / Lyceo in 2026. The old WordPress site goes offline in September 2026. The archive is at `../studiekeuzeadvies archive/`.

The site has two engines:

1. **Found.** 37 city pages and 63 articles answer the question a person types into Google. This is what the purchase paid for. Losing it loses the business.
2. **Sold.** A free intake conversation, then a traject with a personal coach (the old price was €699), or a self-service test package (the old prices were €199 to €499).

Success:
- The rankings survive the move.
- The visitor finds the answer to their search question on the page where they land.
- The chooser or the parent asks for a free intake.
- A good coach writes to us for a city where we have none.

## Brand Personality

Warm and personal. Three words: **human, calm, honest**.

Voice: say "je" and "jij" to everybody, also to parents. Warm, never formal, never childish. Short sentences. Say the difficult part: a wrong study choice costs a year and a lot of money, and we do not always know the answer.

The human in this brand is **the coach**: a named person, with a face, with a region, and with their own history. Not a company, not an 088 number, not a portal.

Emotional goal: the reader must feel less alone and less in a hurry. The stopper must feel that they did not fail.

Never: pressure, urgency, fear about the future, the word "expert" about ourselves, or a number we cannot prove.

## Anti-references

The site must not look like:

- **A corporate consultancy.** Dark blue and gold, a photo of a handshake, "wij ontzorgen". Formal and cold.
- **A government website.** Correct, grey, dense, with no person visible.
- **A SaaS landing page.** Gradient hero, a row of three cards with icons, big number statistics, purple accent, "Get started free".
- **A gamified app.** A mascot, points, badges, a level bar, animation after every action. This is a serious decision, not a game.
- **The old site itself.** A bar of three statistics under the hero (8,8 / 92% / "meest gevraagde partij"), stock photos of pupils in a library, and 37 city pages that are one text with the city name changed.

Watch item, not a ban: the standard Dutch education look, soft blue and mint green with rounded shapes and stock photos of pupils who laugh. Every competitor is there.

## Design Principles

1. **Two readers, one page.** The chooser reads first, the parent reads over their shoulder or later that night. Never make two separate sites. Write the page for the chooser, and answer the parent's questions (what, how long, how much, who) on the same page, in a place they can find fast.
2. **Answer before you offer.** The visitor arrived with one question. Answer it completely and for free. The invitation for a free intake comes after the answer, never before it.
3. **A named coach, not a call centre.** All trust comes from one person with a face, a city, and a real history. A page about a region without a person on it is a page without proof.
4. **Calm is the feature.** The reader is worried and often ashamed. No urgency, no counters, no popups. Space and rhythm do more work than color.
5. **Say only what is true.** No number, no name, and no customer story goes on the site until we know we may use it. Where proof is missing, say less, do not invent more.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Good practice level. No formal WCAG statement and no audit at this time.

Minimum:
- Text contrast of 4.5:1 or better, also for the warm and light colors.
- Full keyboard operation, with a visible focus ring.
- Correct heading order and real landmarks.
- Body text of 17px or more. One reader is 17 and reads in the dark. The other is 45 or more and reads at night.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Simple Dutch. Choosers with ADD, ADHD, or autism are a named user group, so overview and short blocks are a requirement, not a style.

## Inherited Content and Open Questions

The archive at `../studiekeuzeadvies archive/` holds 514 pages, 219 media files, a redirect map, and the Yoast sitemaps.

**The rights are bought.** Confirmed on 2026-08-06: the purchase includes permission to use all content of the old site. Nothing in the archive is blocked by rights. That covers the 514 pages, the 219 media files, the coach biographies and photos, the customer stories, and the numbers.

**Content we can use:**
- 63 articles, 2014 to 2026, of which 19 from 2025. They were orphaned on the old site: no `/blog/` hub, no navigation link, one inbound link each.
- The 37 city pages, as a text base only. They are one template with the city name changed.
- The description of the method, the four meetings, and the product structure.
- The coach biographies and photos (Mirjam, Janneke, Astrid, Barbara, and the others).
- The customer stories on `/ervaringen/`.

**Still open, but not because of rights:**
- The 8,8 coach rating, the 92 percent success number, and "meest gevraagde partij". We may print these sentences. Whether they are true of the new company is a different question, and Design Principle 5 answers it: a number goes on the site when we can prove it of ourselves. Most of the coaches who earned the 8,8 are not ours.
- The Qompas tests (Persoonstype, Competentie, Beroepen, Interesse) and the TalentenTest with Tilburg University. Owning the page that describes a test is not owning the test. The decision of 2026-08-04 is that the site does not sell them.
- The old prices, €199 to €699. They belong to a product that included the tests.
- The meeting rooms. The seller rented them, and a lease is not content. Confirm each address before it goes on a city page.

**Decisions that need data, not judgement:**
- Which of the 63 articles to keep. This needs Search Console, and access disappears with the seller's tenant.
- Which city pages to keep. The rule is: a city page only where a coach works. Every dropped city loses its ranking, so measure first.
- All 522 URLs need a keep, redirect, or drop decision. The 22 redirects that already exist must survive; they carry link value from two or three rebrands ago.
